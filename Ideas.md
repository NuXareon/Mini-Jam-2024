-- Two loop expansion ideas --
Two targets:
- Casual-ish mobile game
- Hardcore pc game

- Progression:
Define different levels, each level would be a sequence of several pseudo-random fights. Levels might have sublevels to help progressiong: 1-1, 1-2, 2-1, 2-1...
Progressing through levels will give the players upgrade points which they can use to updgrade their equipment
After reaching certain level milestones, players will unlock new items and even new slots.
    Note: keep this realistic, ideally no more than 9 items and 4 slots.
Level progression can be traditional (re-start at the beginning of the level), or in a rogue-like maner
Items found might be lost on level restart (rogue-like)
Items can be permanently unlocked, but they might have unlock requirements (tbd which).
    Unlocked once completed the level they were found in (the new found item is unlock)
    Unlocked once defeated a boss (can choose which item to unlock)

- Deck building
At the beggining of the level, the player selects a sub set of items to use during the level. i.e. there are more items than can be used in a single run.

- Item upgrading
Upgrade points can be spend to updagrade an item.
Upgrade will upgrade the existing stats of the item. This upgrades are meant to be small in nature.
Once an item is fully upgraded it will get a new power or stat.
Items can only be upgraded between levels

- Item final updgrade
Initial sword: execute enemies at low health
Sunglasses: make them do something cool. Also the main char should wear them when equipped.
